YT Music Lyrics Router

Routes the song playing on YouTube Music to Hinglish / English lyrics.

Where lyrics open

The side panel never redirects itself — it offers the fallback as a button instead. It needs Chrome 114 or newer; on anything older the extension opens the lyrics tab.

Following the song

LRCLIB stores a timed version of a good share of its catalogue. When the track playing has one, the panel highlights the line that is singing, scrolls to keep it in view, and jumps the player to any line you click. Scrolling yourself hands control back for a few seconds.

The Sync button in the panel turns it off for one song without changing this. Tracks with no timings are shown as plain text exactly as before, and the panel says which of its other matches are timed with a ◍.

Identifying the song

The same song reaches YouTube Music as an original, a lyrical video, a remix and any number of re-uploads, each titled by whoever uploaded it. This looks the song up in Apple's music catalogue and checks the answer against the runtime of what is playing, so the lyrics search gets the real title and artist instead of the uploader's.

Only used when the metadata looks like an upload — a catalogue entry that already knows its own title and artist is left alone. Turning this on asks for permission to contact itunes.apple.com; nothing else changes, and no lyrics come from there. Untick it to revoke the permission again.

When LRCLIB has nothing

LRCLIB is community-maintained and its Hindi catalogue thins out the further back you go. LyricsMint carries a lot of what it misses, in Hinglish, and files songs at a predictable address — so this reads the page for the film and song already identified rather than searching or crawling. It runs only after every LRCLIB attempt has come back empty.

Asks for permission to contact lyricsmint.com; untick to revoke it. Lyrics are copyrighted and LyricsMint licenses them for use on its own site, so this is for your own reading — check their terms before relying on it, and expect it to break if they change their markup.
